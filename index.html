The Problem

You know what you pumped to. You have no idea what it is now. You're developing wings without your most fundamental variable.

Materials stretch. Sun heats the air frame. Spray cools it down. Every gust, every crash, every minute on the water — your pressure is drifting. You've been flying a different wing since the moment you launched. Pressure defines how your wing performs, how your materials behave, and where your designs fail. But today you set a number at the beach and hope it holds. It doesn't. Materials stretch, temperatures shift, conditions change. Every piece of rider feedback, every prototype comparison, every failure analysis — correlated to a number that no longer existed.
